## Decision

Store research as non-governing Markdown reference records under sundial/research with shared domains and a short summary for list views; agents must explicitly open the research file before relying on long-form details.

## Pitfalls

Do not place long API signatures, parameter catalogs, or version-specific research into Decision Record one-line guidance just to make it retrievable.

## Appendix

Remember-research records give future agents durable, sourceable facts without bloating DR retrieval output.
